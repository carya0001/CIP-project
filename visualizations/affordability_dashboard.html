How to read the burden

  • Premium: average monthly premium of an adult in the standard model with CHF 300 deductible, mean over all insurers and then over the premium regions of a canton (each counted equally), × 12.
  • Income: median taxable income of all taxpayers (ESTV). Taxable income is income after deductions and counted per tax unit, so a married couple is one unit.
  • The burden compares one adult's premium with one tax unit's income. It is an indicator for comparing cantons and years, not the real share of a household budget. Premium subsidies are not deducted.
