Who grew since 2012?

Median taxable income as an index, 2012 = 100. Hover a line to pick out a canton.

Geneva is the only canton whose median taxable income was lower in 2022 than in 2012. The Valais 2020 jump (+6.6%) is flagged as a likely change in the source data, not real growth.

Rank by median income

Position among the 26 cantons each year (1 = highest).

The ranking is very stable: the top and bottom groups barely move over eleven years.

Income vs. inequality

Median taxable income against the Gini coefficient. Bubble size = number of taxpayers.

Richer cantons are not automatically more unequal. Basel-Stadt and Geneva combine middling medians with the highest inequality.

Married vs. single taxpayers

A married couple files as one tax unit, so its income is roughly two earners'. ESTV changed the household categories in 2020, so only married and total are comparable across all years.

Gross wages by large region (BFS)

Monthly gross wage, full-time equivalent. Survey every second year.

BFS wages exist only for 7 large regions, not per canton, and correlate only moderately with cantonal taxable income (r ≈ 0.46). That is why the tax data stays our main income measure and BFS is the cross-check.
